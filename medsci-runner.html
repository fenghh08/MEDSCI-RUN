      How to play

      Objective

      You're running through the bloodstream. Steer into topic blocks (↑/↓ or W/S, swipe on mobile) to trigger a multiple-choice question from that topic. Answer enough questions correctly in each required topic to clear the stage — there are 4 clinical stages total.

      Life

      Controls

      ↑/↓ or W/S — switch lanes
      1–6 — pick an MCQ option, Enter — continue after answering
      P / Esc — pause

      Study guide

      Every topic and item you'll be quizzed on is browsable any time from the 📚 button — before, during (it pauses first), or after a run. If there's more than one theme, tabs appear to switch between them. Each question you answer also links back to its study guide entry in your session summary.

      Study & Practice mode

      A separate, untimed way to study: pick a theme, optionally narrow to specific topics and a course, then either browse the filtered guide or run a practice quiz — no glucose, no stages, just questions one after another with immediate feedback. End it anytime to see your summary.

      Group Race

      Race friends on separate devices — one hosts and shares a 5-character room code, everyone else joins with it. Same rules as solo Runner mode, but everyone is ranked by score once all racers are done, and you can see a live leaderboard in the HUD while you play. Elimination pops up a notice for everyone still racing.
